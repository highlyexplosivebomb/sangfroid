Sangfroid is a scavenger hunt. Players complete challenges to earn points. The team with the highest score wins.

Each challenge earns a different number of points, depending on type and difficulty. You determine your strategy. Will you blitz through low-value challenges, or take your time to solve high-value challenges?

## Basic Rules
- All challenges occur within map boundaries in publicly accessible areas.
- If your team has multiple players, you can split up and work on multiple challenges at the same time.
- All tools are allowed (e.g. Google, ChatGPT). But beware - challenges have taken this into consideration, and you might be misled.
- Public transport operated by TFNSW (Train, Bus, Ferry, Light Rail) is allowed. You can't drive, use rideshare, or rent bikes. An automated system will flag suspicious travel patterns, and your team could be disqualified.

Disclaimer: Sangfroid is not endorsed by the City of Sydney or any public/private entities. You must follow the law and respect instructions from authorities. Exercise common sense and never put yourself in danger. You are responsible for all your own actions. If a challenge can't be completed because of extraordinary circumstances, contact John immediately.

## Game Format
A Sangfroid game is 2.5 hours long with 3 stages. 

#### 1. Pre-Game
00:00:00 - 00:05:00

Challenges are revealed. Use this time to discuss a strategy or troubleshoot technical issues.

#### 2. Game
00:05:00 - 02:05:00

Challenges are opened for submission. Unique challenges will appear periodically, so pay attention to pop-ups!

#### 3. Finale
02:05:00 - 02:30:00

Challenges are closed for submission, and a final challenge is revealed. Your team must solve it and reach the finish line before the timer finishes, or 20% of your points will be removed.

## Challenge Types
#### Answer
One correct solution. You have unlimited attempts, but if you answer incorrectly, a 1 minute cooldown applies for that challenge.

#### Photo
Many correct solutions, as long as they meet the challenge's criteria. Submissions are reviewed by admins, which may take a few mins. You can complete other challenges while waiting.

Pay attention to the challenge status.
- Under Review: Your submission is submitted and pending review.
- Rejected: Your submission was rejected and you need to resubmit.
- Approved: Your submission was approved and the challenge is solved.

#### Unique
A twist on traditional scavenger hunts. Follow the challenge's instructions carefully. You might play a minigame, or work with/sabotage other teams.
